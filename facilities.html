Facilities & Services

Desert camping, safaris and comfort

Hotel Victoria Jaisalmer is a high class hotel with camping facilities in the midst of the Thar Desert, surrounded by natural sand dunes. Enjoy comfortable accommodation in mud cottages, huts and tents with campfire, dinner, music, folk dance and a camel ride.

  • High speed internet and e-mail
  • Round the clock room service
  • STD, ISD, CTV
  • Laundry
  • Doctor on call
  • Travel counter
  • Country tour
  • Camel safari
  • Desert tour
  • Overnight safari with camel ride
  • Sunset from the sand dunes
  • Snacks
  • Rajasthani folk music, dance and dinner
  • Overnight in traditional huts and Swiss tents
