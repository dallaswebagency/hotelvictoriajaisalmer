About Jaisalmer

A sleepy desert city turned jewel of the Thar

Once a sleepy desert city, Jaisalmer has transformed itself into a major tourist destination of India. Situated amid the picturesque Thar Desert, it commands a premier position among the tourist attractions of Rajasthan, revered by Indians and foreign tourists alike.

Jaisalmer Fort, locally known as Sonar Quila, is a dominating structure amidst the sands. The city is also known for its old mansions, the havelis, of which Salim Ji ki Haveli and Nathmal Ji ki Haveli are the most striking. The amalgamation of ancient and medieval architecture will catch your fancy.

Jaisalmer is a prominent educational and cultural hub that regularly showcases the vivid culture of Rajasthan, including folk dances and semi classical and folk music. The people are warm and welcoming, so come and enjoy the traditional call of padharo mahare desh.

Padharo mahare desh. Welcome to our land.
