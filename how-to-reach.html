Getting to Jaisalmer

By train, air or road

Distances are approximate, so please confirm travel times before you set out.

✦
🚆

By train

Jaisalmer Railway Station is a short auto or taxi ride from the fort. Trains connect Jaisalmer with cities such as Jodhpur and Delhi.

✈

By air

Check current flights into Jaisalmer. Many guests also fly to Jodhpur, about 285 km away, and continue by road or train.

🚗

By road

Jodhpur is about 285 km away and Bikaner about 330 km. Taxis and buses run to Jaisalmer, and we can help you plan the drive.
