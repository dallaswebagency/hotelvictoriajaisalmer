About Us

The best place to stay in the only living fort of the world

The Golden City of India, Jaisalmer was founded in 1156. It is known for its magnificent sandstone palaces, ornate Jain temple, the finely wrought manors of the palace and the Hotel Victoria. Hotel Victoria is said to be the best place to stay in the only live fort of the world.

It comprises two converted town houses built right into the fort walls, with rooms decorated in vivid shades of crimson, lime and orange. Its three terraces command the finest Jaisalmer sunset views. Hotel Victoria has a simple charm that suits its remote, romantic Arabian Nights setting in the desert town of Jaisalmer.

Its glorious roof terrace, covered with Indian floor cushions, offers a spectacular view of the town, fort and desert beyond. If you are looking for a delightful and affordable vacation, the picturesque city of Jaisalmer is waiting for you.

FAQ

Questions guests often ask

Quick answers about staying inside Jaisalmer Fort.

✦
01Where is Hotel Victoria located?

Hotel Victoria is on the fort, in Kund Para, Jaisalmer 345001, Rajasthan. It sits right inside Jaisalmer Fort, one of the last living forts in the world, so the palace, the havelis and the old town bazaars are all within walking distance.

02How do I book a room?

Use the Book a Room button to check availability and book online, or send us a request on the Make Enquiry page, call us on +91 93747 57770 or +91 2992 252150, or write to hotelvictoria@rediffmail.com. Tell us your dates and number of guests and we will confirm availability and rates.

03What are the rooms like?

Our rooms are spread over two converted town houses built into the fort walls. They are decorated in warm shades of crimson, lime and orange, and each has its own personality. Many have cushioned balconies with views across the fort city.

04Does the hotel have a rooftop terrace and restaurant?

Yes. We have three terraces, including a roof terrace with floor cushions that looks out over the town, the fort and the desert. Meals are served on the terraces, and guests often tell us the sunset views and the Victoria special thali are highlights of their stay.

05Can you arrange desert safaris and tours?

Yes. Our travel counter can arrange camel safaris, Thar Desert tours, sunset on the dunes, a Rajasthani folk music and dinner evening, and overnight stays in the desert in mud huts or Swiss tents.

06What is there to see near the hotel?

Salim Ji ki Haveli, Nathmal Ji ki Haveli and Sonar Quila (the fort itself) are all close by. Our team can suggest walking routes, the best time for photos and where to watch the sunset.

07How do guests rate Hotel Victoria?

Hotel Victoria is rated excellent by 860 travellers on Tripadvisor and has more than 300 five star reviews on Google. Guests regularly mention the location inside the fort, clean rooms, home cooked food and friendly staff.

08How do I find the hotel when I arrive?

The fort has narrow lanes, so please call us before you arrive. Our team will guide you from the fort gate to the hotel.

09Which is the best hotel to stay inside Jaisalmer Fort?

Hotel Victoria is a heritage hotel built into the walls of Jaisalmer Fort. It is rated excellent by 860 travellers on Tripadvisor and has more than 300 five star reviews on Google, with guests praising the location, clean rooms, home cooked food and rooftop views.

10Is there a hotel in Jaisalmer with a rooftop restaurant and sunset view?

Yes. Hotel Victoria has three terraces, including a roof terrace with floor cushions, where you can have dinner and watch the sunset over the fort, the town and the desert.

11Can I book a camel safari and desert camp through the hotel?

Yes. Our travel counter can arrange a camel safari, a desert tour, sunset on the dunes, a folk music and dinner evening and an overnight desert camp. Message us on WhatsApp for timings and rates.
